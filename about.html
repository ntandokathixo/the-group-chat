ABOUT THE GROUP CHAT

Reality TV is rarely a solo experience.

The Group Chat helps you find reality shows worth watching and gives you somewhere to talk about everything that happens afterwards.

MADE FOR THE DEBRIEF

Reality TV does not end when the credits roll. It continues through screenshots, voice notes, theories and very strong opinions.

The Group Chat is a space for discovering your next show and sharing the reactions that come with it. It is made for viewers who take reality TV seriously, but not too seriously.

So pick your mood, find your show and join the chat.
